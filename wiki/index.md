# Knowledge Base Template

Concise, source-traceable knowledge about a regulatory or standards corpus. Replace this page when instantiating the template.

## Browse

- [Law](law/) - Primary legal instruments, article by article, with annexes, secondary legislation and interacting law.
- [Obligations](obligations/) - Role and lifecycle views of what the instrument requires.
- [Standards](standards/) - Standards and specifications referenced by the instrument, as identifiers, lifecycle facts and links.
- [Guidance](guidance/) - Official non-binding guidance from the responsible institutions and authorities.
- [Jurisdictions](jurisdictions/) - National authorities, implementing measures and status per jurisdiction.
- [Timeline](timeline/) - Entry into force, application and transition milestones.
- [Glossary](glossary/) - Terms defined by the instrument.
